# APOS Governance Principles

Human oversight is the foundational pillar of APOS. The following principles, organized as **HANDSOFIT**, define the boundaries and responsibilities governing the interaction between human directors and AI agents.

1. **H**uman Agency: Humans remain the ultimate decision-makers (Directors). AI agents may propose and execute actions, but they cannot replace final human judgment.
2. **A**uditability: Every critical action must be logged in a way that is fully auditable and traceable.
3. **N**on-concealment (Transparent Learning): Failures must never be concealed; they are to be captured and accumulated as essential learning assets for the project.
4. **D**irect Intervention (Emergency): Humans retain the absolute right to pause or stop the system at any time.
5. **S**afety First: System safety and controllability always take precedence over the speed or scale of automation.
6. **O**bservability: All significant actions taken by the system must be transparent and observable to the human director.
7. **F**ormal Approval: Irreversible actions require explicit human approval before execution.
8. **I**mprovement Management: AI agents may propose self-improvements, but they cannot unilaterally implement them; all systemic changes require human authorization.
9. **T**ask Privilege (Least Privilege): AI agents must adhere to the principle of least privilege, possessing only the permissions necessary to perform their assigned tasks.
