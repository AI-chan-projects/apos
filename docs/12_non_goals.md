# Non-Goals

To maintain the project's focus and philosophical integrity, we explicitly define what APOS does not aim to achieve:

1. **General Purpose AGI:** APOS does not aim to build a general-purpose artificial intelligence that solves all of life's problems. It is strictly optimized as a "Meta-Operating System" for managing project lifecycles.

2. **Zero-Human-Control:** We do not aim for full automation without human oversight. Human governance and approval are inherent constraints of the system; a system that excludes human intervention is contrary to our design philosophy.

3. **Black-box Automation:** We do not force the user to validate the internal Chain-of-Thought (CoT) of the AI. Internal CoT is considered an iterative space for trial-and-error to achieve goals, which is left to the system's autonomy. Instead, the system focuses on providing an interface where humans can transparently verify the "final decision," the "actions taken," and the "resulting state changes."

4. **Hardware Optimization:** We do not prioritize hardware-specific or OS-level optimizations. Our priority is to provide abstracted agent interfaces and protocols that can run flexibly across various environments.
